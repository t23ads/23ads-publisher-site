23ADS Internal Video Publisher — operated by 23 STORE Co., Ltd.

Last updated: June 10, 2026

1. Who we are

The 23ADS Internal Video Publisher ("the Tool") is an internal software tool owned and operated by 23 STORE Co., Ltd., 10/130 Moo 2, Khlong Kluea, Pak Kret, Nonthaburi 11120, Thailand. The Tool is used only by our employees and is not offered to the public.

2. Use of YouTube API Services

The Tool uses YouTube API Services (YouTube Data API v3) to upload videos created by our team to our own YouTube channel (23Ads). By using the Tool, users agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

3. What data the Tool accesses, collects, and stores

  • OAuth credentials: the Tool stores a Google OAuth 2.0 access/refresh token for our own channel account. The token is stored locally on a single company-controlled workstation and is used solely to authorize video uploads.
  • Uploaded content: the Tool transmits video files, titles, and descriptions created by our team to YouTube.
  • No third-party user data: the Tool does not access, collect, store, or share any data belonging to other YouTube users or viewers. It does not read comments, analytics of other channels, subscriber lists, or any personal information.
  • No cookies or tracking: the Tool has no public interface and does not use cookies or advertising trackers.

4. Data sharing

We do not sell, share, or transfer any data obtained through YouTube API Services to any third party. Authorized data is processed only on our internal systems.

5. Data retention and deletion

Stored OAuth tokens remain on our internal workstation until revoked or deleted. We delete stored tokens immediately upon revocation of access or when no longer needed.

6. Revoking access

The authorized account holder can revoke the Tool's access to their Google account at any time via the Google security settings page: https://security.google.com/settings/security/permissions.
